Top 20 holders · average entry

Avg entry = total SOL/USDC spent on buys ÷ tokens bought (SOL converted to USD at the hourly price of each buy). Entry MC = avg entry × total supply. “Bought %” = share of acquired tokens that were bought vs received by transfer — a low value means most of the bag arrived for free (dev, insider, airdrop, or a wallet fed by another wallet). Pools, bonding curves and other program-owned accounts are dimmed and skipped.
